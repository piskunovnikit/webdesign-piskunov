Bot questions.

How is the built-to-order bot different from the AI assistant?+

The built-to-order bot works on fixed scenarios — leads, payments, booking, CRM integration. The AI assistant understands natural language and answers from your knowledge base — instead of buttons and commands, it's a real conversation, closer to talking with a team member.

Do I need my own server?+

Yes, a low-cost VPS — that way the bot belongs to you, rather than staying on my hosting indefinitely. I'll help you choose and set one up, then deploy and configure the bot on it myself — you only pay the hosting provider directly.

I don't have a precise spec, what then?+

That's fine. We'll talk through your task and figure out together what's worth automating first.

How long does it take?+

Depends on the number of integrations and how complex the logic is — I'll confirm the exact timeline after we discuss the task.

How does payment work?+

No mandatory 100% upfront payment. Work and payment are split into clear stages that work for both sides: you see the intermediate result, sign off on it, and pay for that part.
