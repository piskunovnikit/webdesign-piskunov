01

About the business

Ivanhoe Sea Safaris — a whale-watching operator in Gansbaai, South Africa. Running since 1996, the only operator departing directly from Gansbaai harbour.

02

Task

Build a modern site that earns trust and a sense of safety. Make booking clear and fast.

03

Goal

Attracting tourists, raising the share of online bookings, showcasing the company’s years of experience. A feeling of professionalism and safety.

04

Audience

Tourists planning a trip to South Africa, to the Cape Town area. They value easy booking and a clear price, and look for a trusted operator.

Solution

The ocean is the main argument.

A large photo of the ocean takes the center of the composition. A deep blue backdrop and a light turquoise accent keep the sense of openness and trust.

Every screen lets you pick a trip and a date, if the decision is already made. A visitor can calmly weigh the options and book a spot.
