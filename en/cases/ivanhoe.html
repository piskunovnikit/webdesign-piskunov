CASE STUDY · WHALE WATCHING

A slow site with a full-screen video was replaced by a fast path to booking.

Ivanhoe Sea Safaris — a whale-watching operator in Gansbaai, South Africa. The only operator departing directly from Gansbaai harbour.

Open the mockup → Discuss your project
Task

Speed up the site and build a clear path to booking a tour

What I did

Design, layout, structure — a booking-focused mockup

Result (PageSpeed)

35 → 73 mobile performance score

Problem

The original site literally wouldn't open on a phone

Full-size, uncompressed photos, a video running on a JS slider in the hero background, a duplicated menu. PageSpeed scored it 35 out of 100 on mobile — the site would sometimes just stop responding.

Solution

A booking-focused mockup inspired by Apple + Airbnb + GetYourGuide

Built the interface around a single scenario — from the first screen to booking:

  • Currency switcher: ZAR / USD / EUR
  • A sticky price bar at the bottom of the screen through every step of scrolling
  • An FAQ with honest wording — no invented "100% whale sighting" numbers
  • Every piece of copy is a verbatim quote from the client's original site — nothing made up
