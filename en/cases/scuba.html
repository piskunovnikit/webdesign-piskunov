CASE STUDY · DIVE CENTER

A tired template and murky photos gave way to a cinematic, Ferrari-inspired design.

Scuba Magic — a dive school and gear shop in Roodepoort, South Africa. Operating since January 2002, founded by Robin & Lyn Cooke.

Open the mockup → Discuss your project
Task

Replace an outdated layout and tiny 150×138px thumbnail photos

What I did

Design, layout, photo generation, animated price counter

Style

Sharp corners, full-bleed photos, a rare red accent — a Ferrari-inspired pattern

Problem

The site hadn't changed in years, and the gallery photos were 150×138px thumbnails

Dated fonts, a random photo grid, and photo quality in the original gallery that wasn't fit for a full-size mockup — just small, washed-out thumbnails with no link to the original.

Before — scubamagic.co.za
Solution

A cinematic concept with custom-generated photography

Used Ferrari's design system as a reference — not the brand, just the techniques:

  • Sharp, un-rounded corners, near-black background, a rare red accent
  • A full-bleed hero photo across the whole screen
  • Courses presented as a spec table with an animated price counter on scroll
  • 10 photos generated via Gemini (Nano Banana) to replace low-quality originals
  • A real Google map and rating — 4.5★, 19 reviews, with a genuine review quote
