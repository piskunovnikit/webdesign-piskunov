01

About the business

Scuba Magic — a recognized NAUI dive school and gear shop in Roodepoort, South Africa. Running since 2002, founded by Robin & Lyn Cooke.

02

Task

Build a site that conveys the feeling of diving and earns trust in the instructors before the first call. Make the structure clear so a visitor quickly finds the right course.

03

Goal

Attracting new students and raising the number of course inquiries, showcasing the school’s expertise.

04

Audience

People in and around Johannesburg who want to learn diving or service their gear. They value a personal approach and trust in the instructor.

Solution

Cinematic feel and the emotion of diving.

A full-screen photo of a diver on a coral reef pulls you into the atmosphere right away, the feeling that the ocean starts at the screen. Warm tones and vivid underwater shots create a sense of invitation, not a sales pitch.

The founders' story, the course lineup, and the photo gallery follow one another naturally, like chapters of one story.
