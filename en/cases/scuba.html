01

About the business

Scuba Magic — a dive school and gear shop in Roodepoort, South Africa. Running since January 2002, founded by Robin & Lyn Cooke, a recognized NAUI school.

02

Task

Build a site that conveys the feeling of diving and earns trust in the instructors before the first call. Make the structure clear so a visitor quickly finds the right course.

03

Goal

Help attract new students and raise the number of course inquiries, showcase the school's expertise.

04

Audience

People aged 20–50 in and around Johannesburg who want to learn diving or service their gear. They value a personal approach and trust in the instructor.

Solution

The diver is the main argument.

A large photo of a diver in a shaft of sunlight fills the entire first screen. A dark backdrop, a rare red accent, and the texture of water keep the cinematic feel, borrowed from how expensive sports brands present themselves.

Every screen has a clear next step, but the interface never pushes: a visitor can calmly browse the courses and sign up.
